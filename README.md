# DistroTrack

### Distribution Management System for Wholesale FMCG Businesses

DistroTrack is a modern distribution management platform designed to simplify and digitize the day-to-day operations of wholesale FMCG distributors.

It connects the entire distribution workflow — from importing picklists and managing products to assigning deliveries, tracking workers, recording payments, managing parties, and monitoring business performance.

Built with a focus on **simplicity, reliability, and real-world usability**, DistroTrack is designed for businesses that currently depend heavily on manual paperwork and disconnected processes.

---

## 🚀 What is DistroTrack?

Traditional distribution businesses often manage their operations using paper picklists, spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual records.

DistroTrack brings these processes together into one centralized platform.

The system allows administrators to:

- Import and manage picklists
- Manage products and inventory
- Manage parties/customers
- Create and manage delivery routes
- Assign workers to routes
- Track deliveries
- Record cash, cheque, and credit payments
- Monitor outstanding payments
- Track worker activity
- Manage business expenses
- View reports and analytics
- Maintain complete delivery history

Workers receive their assigned deliveries through a simplified interface designed specifically for field operations.
